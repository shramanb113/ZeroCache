# Aurora Cloud Storage Pricing

The Free tier includes 5GB of storage and 10GB of monthly egress at no cost. The Pro tier is $12/month and includes 750GB of storage and 1.5TB of monthly egress. The Business tier is usage-based: $0.018/GB stored per month and $0.045/GB for egress beyond the first 2TB, billed monthly in arrears. There are no charges for API requests on any tier.
